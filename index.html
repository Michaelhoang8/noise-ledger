Mechanism & interpretation

Each output adds the difference of two geometric counts sampled with independent cryptographic random draws. The resulting integer noise has probability proportional to q^|k|. A count or fixed-bin histogram has add/remove sensitivity 1 when each person contributes one record to one bin. Each release costs ε = ln(1/q).

Budget accounting multiplies the exact fractions 1/q and compares that product to the sealed budget factor. Logarithms are used only for display. Counts may be negative or exceed the source size; no clipping or truncation is applied. Repeated canonical queries reuse their stored result.

Budgets are per sealed dataset. Overlapping people across different datasets are not composed together. Re-sealing identical records, even renamed or reordered, reuses the existing budget; altered records start a distinct dataset. Do not treat this operator app, its timing, or its exact-count views as a production privacy boundary.

Ledger hashes detect corruption, not an attacker who can rewrite the database and recompute hashes. Randomness is not recorded or seeded for replay; cached outputs survive process restarts. Only the noisy output and release metadata are included in export buttons.
